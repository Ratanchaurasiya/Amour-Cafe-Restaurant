OUR PHILOSOPHY

A Symphony of Warmth, Privacy & Fine Gastronomy

Conceived as an oasis of refined dining on Balpur Road near the Power House, Amour Cafe & Restaurant brings metropolitan cafe elegance and royal North Indian culinary heritage to Tarkulwa.

Whether welcoming couples seeking acoustic privacy in secluded cabins, families gathering over rich slow-simmered gravies, or friends celebrating milestone birthdays, every encounter at Amour is crafted with care.
